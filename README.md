# Karnataka-Yatra-ai
Karnataka Yatra AI is an intelligent tourism platform that helps travellers explore Karnataka’s waterfalls, beaches, hill stations, heritage sites, and local cuisine. It offers AI-powered trip planning, personalised itineraries, interactive maps, transport and accommodation guidance, weather updates, and organise tourism information.
**Karnataka Yatra AI** is an AI-powered tourism and smart travel platform designed to transform how people discover and explore Karnataka. It combines destination discovery, personalised trip planning, regional food exploration, interactive maps and travel information in one unified application.

The platform helps travellers explore waterfalls, beaches, hill stations, historical monuments, wildlife sanctuaries and cultural attractions across Karnataka. Its AI-powered trip planner generates personalised itineraries based on users' budgets, travel duration, interests and preferences. The AI travel assistant provides destination recommendations, answers travel-related questions and helps users modify their itineraries. Additional features include restaurant discovery, accommodation information, transport guidance, route planning and weather updates through supported integrations.

A unique feature, the **AI Webstrapping Studio**, assists administrators in researching destinations, organising information from permitted sources, analysing images and preparing structured tourism content for human verification before publication.

The proposed technology stack includes React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Python, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, PostGIS and configurable AI services. Interactive maps, cinematic 3D visuals, responsive navigation and smooth animations create an engaging user experience.

Karnataka Yatra AI aims to simplify travel planning, promote local tourism, highlight regional cuisine and improve destination discovery through intelligent technology. Its long-term vision is to become a comprehensive digital travel companion connecting Karnataka's natural beauty, heritage, culture and experiences with personalised, accessible and reliable travel planning.
